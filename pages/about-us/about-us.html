ABOUT US

One of excellence and experience. Since our inception, All Door Tech's philosophy has been unwavering: to provide every customer with a superior experience in the repair and maintenance of doors, gates, and loading dock equipment. We are a locally owned and operated company with over 22 years of experience in the industry. Our identity is based on being a credible and reliable full-service provider specializing in commercial and industrial solutions for all types of entry systems.

Mission

Our mission is to provide our clients with specialized attention and services of the highest quality at competitive prices. We are dedicated to helping businesses minimize costs and maintain a safe working environment through early detection of equipment issues, saving both time and money. As AAADM certified professionals, our mission is to optimize the performance of every component we serve.

Vision

To consolidate ourselves as the leading reference in preventive maintenance and repair of access systems in Southern California, expanding our reach in the counties of Riverside, San Bernardino, Los Angeles, Orange, San Diego, and Imperial.
